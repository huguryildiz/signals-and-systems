What the course covers

The material runs from the definition of a signal to the sampling theorem. Continuous time and discrete time are developed side by side and kept visibly separate, and every result states the conditions it depends on.

Energy and power are normalised at one ohm, the imaginary unit is j, and sinc is unnormalised. Each convention is restated where it is used, so no page assumes you remember a choice made three chapters earlier.
